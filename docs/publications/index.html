About These Editions

Each volume contains every published work in its genre, sorted from highest to lowest rated. Every story includes its full reading apparatus: the combination formula that seeded it, the complete author meeting that explored its themes, and every blind reader review.

The ebook editions are DRM-free EPUB files, readable on any device. Print editions are 6"×9" trade paperbacks. Audio editions are narrated by a single voice, with the author meetings and selected reviews included as supplementary material.
