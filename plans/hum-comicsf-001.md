# Story Plan: hum-comicsf-001

**Genre**: humor-satire / comic-fantasy-sf
**AuthorA**: Douglas Adams (cosmic absurdism; bureaucratic nightmare; British deadpan)
**AuthorB**: Terry Pratchett (satirical wit; moral seriousness beneath absurdism)
**WorkX**: *The Left Hand of Darkness* (Le Guin) — lone envoy on alien world; incomprehensible politics
**WorkY**: *Catch-22* (Heller) — bureaucratic logic devouring reason; circular escalation
**Target**: 3,100 words (2,800-3,400)
**Risk Card**: #3 — STORY-AS-DOCUMENT (official report, diplomatic briefing, or regulatory filing — form IS the satire)

## Writing Samples

### Sample 1: Douglas Adams (Author A)
**Source**: *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*, Chapter 1
**Why representative**: Perfect deadpan escalation of bureaucratic absurdity — the form technically satisfied while the substance is annihilated.

> "But the plans were on display..." "On display? I eventually had to go down to the cellar to find them." "That's the display department." "With a flashlight." "Ah, well, the lights had probably gone." "So had the stairs." "But look, you found the notice, didn't you?" "Yes," said Arthur, "yes I did. It was on display in the bottom of a locked filing cabinet stuck in a disused lavatory with a sign on the door saying 'Beware of the Leopard.'"

Adams's signature moves: the stacking of increasingly absurd qualifiers delivered with matter-of-fact calm, the bureaucratic voice that treats the monstrous as procedural, the parenthetical digressions that become more important than the main clause. His cosmic scale always resolves into mundane discomfort. Vogons "wouldn't save their own grandmothers from the Ravenous Bugblatter Beast of Traal without orders signed in triplicate, sent in, sent back, queried, lost, found, subjected to public enquiry, lost again, and finally buried in soft peat for three months and recycled as firelighters."

### Sample 2: Terry Pratchett (Author B)
**Source**: Characteristic Pratchett voice across Discworld, particularly *Going Postal* and the Watch novels
**Why representative**: Shows the moral anger beneath the comedy, the footnotes-as-worldbuilding, and the sharp turn from joke to knife.

Pratchett's signature is the joke that reveals it was never a joke. His bureaucracies are funny because they're recognizable — the Ankh-Morpork Post Office doesn't just fail to deliver mail, it has developed an entire institutional culture around not delivering mail while maintaining meticulous records of the mail it hasn't delivered. The comedy comes from the gap between the institution's self-description and its actual function. His prose walks a tightrope: warm, chatty, British — then a sentence lands like a slap. "The truth may be out there, but the lies are inside your head." "Taxation is not theft. It's not a conditions-of-membership thing. It's because they'll throw you in prison." He uses footnotes not for jokes but for the quiet details that make the world feel occupied. The humor serves the moral argument, never the reverse.

### Sample 3: *The Left Hand of Darkness* (Work X — Le Guin)
**Source**: Opening paragraph and field-report chapters
**Why representative**: The envoy's voice — formal, self-aware about its own limitations, trying to render the incomprehensible in institutional language.

> "I'll make my report as if I told a story, for I was taught as a child on my homeworld that Truth is a matter of the imagination. The soundest fact may fail or prevail in the style of its telling: like that singular organic jewel of our seas, which grows brighter as one woman wears it and, worn by another, dulls and goes to dust. Facts are no more solid, coherent, round, and real than pearls are."

Le Guin's envoy chapters alternate between field reports (formal, anthropological) and subjective narrative. Genly Ai's attempts to classify Gethenian society keep failing because his categories are wrong — not inadequate but *wrong*, built on assumptions so deep he can't see them. "One is respected and judged only as a human being. It is an appalling experience." The political chapters render two nations locked in a rivalry neither can explain. The structure: multiple document types (myths, reports, legends) that each tell a truth the narrative voice cannot.

### Sample 4: *Catch-22* (Work Y — Heller)
**Source**: The logic of Catch-22 itself and characteristic escalation patterns
**Why representative**: Circular bureaucratic reasoning that is simultaneously hilarious and lethal.

Catch-22's central mechanism: "There was only one catch and that was Catch-22, which specified that a concern for one's own safety in the face of dangers that were real and immediate was the process of a rational mind. Orr was crazy and could be grounded. All he had to do was ask; and as soon as he did, he would no longer be crazy and would have to fly more missions." The loop is self-sealing. Every attempt to escape the system proves you're sane enough to remain in it. Heller's prose runs on accumulation — the same situation restated with slight variations until the repetition itself becomes the horror. The missions keep increasing. The bureaucracy metastasizes. "The people running it don't even notice, because from their perspective, everything is functioning correctly." Comedy as anesthesia until the rug-pull.

## Wikipedia Concepts

1. **COSPAR Planetary Protection Categories** — The real-world Committee on Space Research classifies celestial bodies into contamination risk tiers (I-V), determining what sterilization protocols a spacecraft must follow before visiting. Category V (restricted Earth return) requires the most stringent controls. A bureaucratic framework for deciding how much a world "matters" based on its potential to harbor life — eerily parallel to this story's compliance assessment that determines whether a civilization counts as a civilization.
   **Integration**: The compliance form uses a tiered classification system directly modeled on COSPAR categories. The planet starts as Category III (requires assessment), and the form's logic works to reclassify it downward to Category I (no interest, no protections needed). The envoy recognizes the parallel to planetary protection protocols — a system designed to prevent contamination being used, in effect, to reclassify contamination as terrain.

2. **Linnaean Taxonomy and the "Sexual System"** — Linnaeus classified all plants by their reproductive organs, which many contemporaries called absurd because two plants with the same number of stamens might have nothing else in common. His rival Buffon derided the whole enterprise of taxonomy as artificial. Yet the system endured because it was *usable*, not because it was accurate.
   **Integration**: The alien civilization's social structure is organized around a principle the form cannot classify — something analogous to Linnaeus's problem. The dropdown menu for "Primary Social Organization" offers options like "hierarchical," "democratic," "theocratic," "tribal" — but the actual organizing principle of this civilization maps to none of them. The envoy tries "other" and discovers "other" requires a justification of no more than 40 characters. The civilization's 3,000-year-old social system must fit into a text field designed for edge cases.

## 1. Premise

A Galactic Compliance Assessor files a routine Habitability & Sentience Verification Report (Form GSR-4117, Revision 12.3) on an alien world whose civilization doesn't fit any of the form's dropdown menus. The report is perfectly structured. Every section is completed. Every mandatory field is filled. And the cumulative effect of the form's logic is to reclassify a living civilization as uninhabited terrain, clearing it for resource extraction. The assessor can see this happening and cannot stop it, because the only tool available for objecting is another form.

## 2. Protagonist

- **Name**: Sabil Okonkwo, age 41, Galactic Compliance Assessor, Grade 7 (of 12)
- **WANTS**: To file an accurate report and return to her housing allocation on Relay Station Kepler-9, where she has a lease renewal pending that requires a completed assignment stamp
- **NEEDS**: To acknowledge that accuracy within this system is impossible — that the form's version of truth and actual truth have diverged beyond reconciliation
- **FLAW**: Sabil believes the system can be made to work if you fill it out carefully enough. She has spent 14 years filing reports and has never had one rejected. She mistakes compliance for competence. Her faith in the form is genuine and total — not cynicism, not laziness, but the particular blindness of a person who has succeeded within a broken system and therefore cannot see the breakage.
- **WOUND**: Three years ago she filed an ambiguous report on Kepler-442b, noting "insufficient data" in Section 9. The report was returned with a formal citation for "incomplete assessment methodology." She was demoted from Grade 8. She has not filed an ambiguous report since.

## 3. Structure

**Document format**: The entire story is presented as Form GSR-4117 — a Habitability & Sentience Verification Report. Sections, subsections, mandatory fields, dropdown selections, free-text boxes, footnotes, marginal annotations by a supervisor (in italics), and one appendix. The form IS the narrative. Sabil's voice emerges through the free-text fields, where she is supposed to provide "brief supporting observations" but where her language keeps slipping from bureaucratic into something more human — then catching itself and returning to compliance.

### Beat 1: Opening — Sections 1-3 (Header, Mission Parameters, Planetary Overview)
Standard form fields: assessor name, grade, assignment date, planet designation (KOI-4878.01, "locally designated: [untranslatable — see footnote 3]"), atmospheric composition, surface temperature range. Everything normal. The only hint of trouble: Footnote 3, where Sabil notes that the planet's inhabitants have a name for their world that exists as a harmonic frequency rather than a word, and that the form's "local designation" field has a 30-character limit. She has entered "SEE APPENDIX A" and a supervisor's marginal note reads: *Appendix A exceeds the supplementary character allocation. Please summarize or omit.*

### Beat 2: The Assessment Begins to Crack — Sections 4-6 (Biosphere Classification, Sentience Indicators, Social Organization)
Section 4 (Biosphere): The COSPAR-derived classification system. Sabil selects Category III (potential biotic activity requiring assessment). Her free-text notes describe a biosphere that defies the tiers — organisms that are simultaneously geological and biological, "mineral formations that exhibit responsive behavior consistent with consciousness but inconsistent with any listed sentience indicator." The dropdown for "Primary Life Form Classification" offers: carbon-based, silicon-based, hybrid, synthetic, unknown. The actual answer is "yes" — all of them, simultaneously, depending on the time of day. She selects "unknown" and the form auto-generates a warning: *Classification 'unknown' triggers Extended Protocol 7. Estimated additional assessment time: 340 standard days. Confirm extended assignment? Y/N.*

She selects N.

She changes her answer to "carbon-based" and adds a footnote.

Section 5 (Sentience Indicators): The Linnaean taxonomy problem. The form asks for "Primary Social Organization" with dropdown options. The civilization's organizing principle — a communal harmonic resonance that shifts with geological cycles — maps to none of them. The "other" field allows 40 characters. She types: "Resonance-based collective. See Appx A." Supervisor's note: *Appendix A has been flagged for exceeding supplementary character allocation. Revise or remove reference.*

Section 6 (Social Organization Detail): Multiple-choice questions designed for hierarchies. "Head of state: [name/title]." The civilization has no head of state. It has something closer to a shared mood. The form does not have a field for shared mood. Sabil writes "N/A — governance is distributed" and the form auto-populates a compliance flag: *Absence of identifiable governance structure is consistent with Pre-Sentient classification. Confirm reclassification? Y/N.*

### Beat 3: Escalation — Sections 7-9 (Economic Assessment, Threat Evaluation, Compliance Determination)
Each section ratchets the civilization further from recognition. Section 7 asks about trade capacity (the civilization doesn't trade — it shares resonance patterns that function as both art and resource distribution, but the form interprets "no trade" as "no economy" as "no development" as "pre-sentient"). Section 8 (Threat Evaluation) asks whether the civilization poses a risk to Galactic interests. Sabil writes "none," which the form interprets as "no defensive capability," which triggers a resource-extraction eligibility flag. Section 9 is the compliance determination itself — the section where her Kepler-442b demotion happened. The Catch-22: accurate reporting triggers Extended Protocol (340 additional days, lease expires, housing lost) while inaccurate reporting generates the "correct" classification. Every honest answer pushes the civilization toward reclassification. Every compliant answer makes the report acceptable.

### Beat 4: Crisis — Section 10 (Free-Text Assessment Summary)
The only section with no dropdown menus, no mandatory format. Just a text box: "Provide a summary assessment in 500 words or fewer." This is where Sabil's voice breaks through. She begins in regulation prose and it starts to fracture. She describes what she actually saw: the harmonic structures, the geological-biological entities that sang to her survey equipment in frequencies that made the instruments weep error codes. She describes the moment she understood the civilization was not pre-sentient but so far beyond the form's conception of sentience that the form's categories had wrapped around and classified it as nothing. She writes 847 words. The form truncates at 500.

### Beat 5: Ending — Section 11 (Assessor Certification) and Appendix A
Section 11: checkbox. "I certify that this report is accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge." She checks it. Because it is. The form is accurate and complete. The form is also a lie. Both of these are true. Below the checkbox, her signature, her grade, the date.

Appendix A: The supplementary document that has been flagged, referenced, and rejected throughout. It contains Sabil's actual description of the civilization — 1,200 words of precise, awed, Le Guin-style observation. It has been formally noted as exceeding the supplementary character allocation. It will be archived but not reviewed. It exists in the file but not in the assessment.

The final line: a system-generated stamp. "ASSESSMENT COMPLETE. CLASSIFICATION: CATEGORY I — UNINHABITED TERRAIN. RESOURCE EXTRACTION ELIGIBILITY: CONFIRMED. FILING DATE: [auto-populated]. NEXT REVIEW: NOT APPLICABLE."

## 4. Key Scenes

- **The dropdown moment** (Section 5): Sabil trying to fit "resonance-based collective governance" into 40 characters. The comedy of the constraint. The horror beneath the comedy. She tries multiple phrasings, each truncated. She settles for "Distributed. Non-standard. Harmonic." — which communicates nothing and satisfies the form.

- **The N/Y toggle** (Section 4): The moment she changes "unknown" to "carbon-based" to avoid Extended Protocol 7. Her lease renewal, her housing, her career — all pressing on the toggle. One letter. The gap between what she knows and what she enters. The internal justification: "Carbon is present. They are based. The statement is not technically false."

- **Section 10's truncation**: She finally writes honestly and the form cuts her off at 500 words. The reader has been watching the form contain her; now the form literally silences her. The 347 words that were cut contained the only accurate description of what she found.

- **Appendix A's ghost**: Referenced five times throughout the document. Flagged once. Noted as non-compliant twice. Never read. It is the only true thing in the file and it has been procedurally annihilated.

## 5. Emotional Trajectory

The reader begins **amused** — the form is funny, the dropdown menus are recognizably absurd, the bureaucratic language is a familiar comedy. By Section 6, the amusement acquires an **edge** — the form's logic is starting to produce real consequences. By Section 9, the reader feels **dread disguised as procedure** — the Catch-22 has closed and the comedy has become the mechanism of destruction. Section 10 provides a moment of **raw honesty truncated by format** — not catharsis but its interruption. The ending delivers **institutional finality** — a system-generated stamp that carries more weight than anything a human wrote in the document. The reader finishes feeling **the specific nausea of watching a machine work correctly**.

## 6. Formula Integration

- **Adams (Author A)**: Most visible in the form's self-referential absurdities — the footnotes about footnotes, the auto-generated warnings, the regulations that reference regulations. The cosmic scale reduced to filing requirements. Sections 1-3 and the marginal annotations are Adams territory: deadpan, stacking, the monstrous treated as administrative.

- **Pratchett (Author B)**: Most visible in Section 10 and the moral weight beneath the comedy. Pratchett's knife-turn — the moment the joke reveals it was never a joke — lands when the form reclassifies the civilization. The warm, human voice of Sabil pushing against the form's language is Pratchett's signature: the decent person inside the indecent system.

- **Le Guin / *Left Hand of Darkness* (Work X)**: Most visible in Appendix A and the descriptions of the alien civilization. The lone envoy who understands just enough to know the framework is wrong. The civilization that is genuinely, irreducibly alien — not hostile, not primitive, just built on assumptions so different that the assessment framework cannot perceive them. Le Guin's field-report voice infuses the passages where Sabil tries to describe what she actually encountered.

- **Heller / *Catch-22* (Work Y)**: Most visible in Section 9 and the Catch-22 structure of the compliance determination. Accurate reporting triggers Extended Protocol (career death). Inaccurate reporting triggers reclassification (civilizational death). The circular logic is self-sealing. The system doesn't malfunction — it functions exactly as designed. Heller's escalation pattern runs through the entire form: each section worsens the outcome through mechanisms that are individually reasonable.

## 7. Title

**Compliance Is Voluntary and Has Always Been Completed**

Wait — this title already exists in the corpus. Checking...

Yes, it's in the existing titles list. Need a different title.

**Form GSR-4117 (Revision 12.3): Habitability & Sentience Verification Report, KOI-4878.01**

This is the document's actual title — the form number. It works because: (a) it signals the story-as-document risk card immediately, (b) it's unlike any other title in the corpus, (c) it doesn't start with "The," (d) the clinical bureaucratic language creates anticipatory irony — the reader knows this is humor-satire and the form title promises a collision between official language and something uncontainable.

**Alternate (shorter)**: **Appendix A (Not Reviewed)**

This is the ghost in the document — the appendix that contains the truth and is procedurally ignored. As a title it signals the story's core tragedy while remaining enigmatic to a browser. It foregrounds the thing the form destroys rather than the form itself.

**Final choice**: **Appendix A (Not Reviewed)**

Reasons: More evocative, more compact, creates curiosity. The parenthetical works as both bureaucratic notation and quiet devastation. Does not start with "The." Structurally distinct from all 90+ existing titles.
