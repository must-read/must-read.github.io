## Evaluation Criteria

Each pitch was evaluated on five criteria:
1. **Premise Quality** -- Originality, emotional resonance, narrative potential in short form
2. **Formula Strength** -- How productively the AuthorA/AuthorB/WorkX/WorkY combination generates creative tension
3. **Genre Fit** -- How well the suggested genre/subgenre serves the premise
4. **Distinctiveness** -- How different this pitch is from others in the pool and from existing works
5. **Source Material Richness** -- How much the source material provides concrete detail for the story

Additional factors:
- Genres with fewer existing works received a bonus (western: 6, magical-realism: 6, philosophical-fiction: 6)
- Pitches covering NEW or uncovered subgenres received a bonus
- Pitches in saturated genres/subgenres (literary-fiction: 8 works, horror: 8, fantasy: 8) were held to a higher bar
