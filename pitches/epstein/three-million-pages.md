## Idea

A GS-12 records clerk at the Department of Justice is tasked with processing the release of 3 million pages of documents related to a dead billionaire's sex trafficking case. She must review each page for redactions. The redaction guidelines are 47 pages long and internally contradictory. Names that appear in public court records must be redacted "for privacy." Names that were never in court records cannot be redacted because "they were never part of the investigation." She discovers that the guidelines, if followed precisely, produce the same result as releasing nothing at all. Her supervisor tells her this is working as intended. She files a complaint. The complaint is redacted.

## Source Material

The December 2025 and January 2026 DOJ document releases totaled 3+ million pages, "heavily redacted, missing deadline by weeks. Names withheld 'for privacy reasons.'" The Epstein Files Transparency Act passed 427-1 mandating release within 300 days, yet the implementation produced documents so redacted that critics called them meaningless. Meanwhile, the DOJ conducted surveillance of Congressional offices investigating the files -- the agency tracking the people trying to read what the agency was mandated to release.

## Why These Authors/Works

- **Vonnegut's** simple, declarative sentences delivering devastating understatement -- "so it goes" applied to bureaucratic obstruction; the humanist rage beneath the deadpan
- **Heller's** Catch-22 logic -- the circular, escalating bureaucratic absurdism where every rule exists to negate another rule; the system that cannot be beaten because it is designed to be unbeatable
- **Catch-22** (Heller): the original bureaucratic nightmare where the rules that govern you are the rules that trap you; the institution that exists to perpetuate itself rather than accomplish its purpose
- **The Pale King** (David Foster Wallace): the existential horror of bureaucratic work; the clerk who must process infinite documents knowing the processing is the point, not the content; boredom as a form of institutional violence

## Core Tension

The clerk is not cynical. She believes in transparency. She follows the guidelines with genuine diligence. And the guidelines, followed perfectly, produce perfect opacity. The comedy is structural: a democracy that votes 427-1 for transparency and then builds a machine that converts transparency into its opposite.
