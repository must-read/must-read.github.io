## Idea

A governor's chief of staff receives a call from a former colleague -- now a governor of another state -- asking him to "just look at" a pardon petition for a convicted sex offender who is "a friend of many friends." The story follows the chief of staff over seventy-two hours as he moves the request through the back channels of the governor's office, watching each person he hands it to react with the same pattern: initial revulsion, then consideration, then the quiet arithmetic of political cost. No one says yes. No one says no. The petition is returned without action, but the fact that it was considered -- that it moved through the system, was read, weighed, and only rejected on political calculation rather than moral refusal -- is the story's center.

## Source Material

In 2010, Epstein sought a gubernatorial pardon from Florida Governor Charlie Crist (R), using Bill Richardson (D, New Mexico Governor) as intermediary. Crist's office considered it but ultimately did not grant it. The source notes this "demonstrated Epstein's confidence in political protection" -- a bipartisan confidence, given that a Democratic governor served as intermediary for a request to a Republican governor on behalf of a convicted sex offender.

## Why These Authors/Works

- **Morrison's** incantatory prose and deep engagement with how power operates through relationships and silence -- the way the request moves through the office like a haunting; the weight of what is not said
- **Ishiguro's** restrained narration and unreliable memory -- the chief of staff telling this story years later, adjusting the details, remembering the political calculations but not the moral ones, the quiet devastation of a life lived in service to power
- **An American Tragedy** (Dreiser): the crushing institutional weight that determines outcomes; the individual swept along by forces they understand but cannot resist; class and power as the real protagonists
- **Atonement** (McEwan): the irrevocable act that seems small at the time; the story told in retrospect by someone who understands their own complicity but frames it as inevitability; the narrative as an attempt at absolution that fails

## Core Tension

No pardon was granted. The system "worked." But the story reveals that the system working means a convicted child predator's pardon request was treated as a routine political calculation by two governors from different parties, and rejected not because it was monstrous but because it was inexpedient.
