## Premise
A brilliant software engineer building the core architecture for a predictive AI system lives in a San Francisco apartment optimized for productivity: no art, no plants, meal delivery calibrated to macros, cable management as aesthetic practice. He retreats nightly into a gaming guild where he is someone named Wei_37. When his parents in Shenzhen hint that a neighbor was questioned by authorities -- possibly because of data his own system exported -- he must reckon with the fact that "clean architecture" is never neutral.

## Source Inspiration
Kevin Zhou from Book 1 -- the engineer whose apartment is "a study in optimization," who fixes temporal weighting bugs in preference modeling while his emotional life atrophies. His failed date with Michelle from marketing, his gaming alias Wei_37, his parents' coded warnings from Shenzhen. The critical essay argues through Kevin that "technological neutrality is itself an ideological position, one that serves power."

## Why These Formula Elements
- **AuthorA (William Gibson)**: Gibson's noir-inflected, densely textured near-future prose -- the brand-name realism, the texture of technology as lived environment -- is the right register for Kevin's world. The apartment should feel like a Gibson interior: everything designed, nothing lived in.
- **AuthorB (Rachel Cusk)**: Cusk's austere, essayistic observation provides the emotional register. Her stripped-down dialogue frames -- conversations that reveal everything the speakers cannot say -- suit Kevin's interactions with parents, coworkers, the date that fails.
- **WorkX (Microserfs)**: The structural DNA of tech-worker isolation rendered as both comedy and tragedy. Coupland's gift was making the loneliness of code culture specific and human. Kevin's story should have that quality of recognizing oneself in a cubicle.
- **WorkY (Severance)**: The thematic DNA of performing routine within a system that is actively destroying the world. Ling Ma's protagonist kept going to work after the apocalypse; Kevin keeps optimizing preference models while his family becomes collateral.

## Notes
The story could cover a single 36-hour coding sprint, with the Shenzhen call arriving in the middle. The gaming guild scenes provide the only space where Kevin has a self -- but even that self is mediated through a system. The "withheld information" risk card would work: the reader should understand what Kevin's system does before Kevin allows himself to.
