## Premise
A documentary filmmaker spends one day preparing for a meeting with a whistleblower who claims to have evidence of what a tech corporation is "planning next." Between encrypted messages and childcare logistics, she reviews footage of climate migrants, argues with a foundation that wants her to remove corporate accountability from her latest film, and fields a call from her mother in London who thinks the world needs beauty more than sadness. By evening, she has not yet decided whether to take the meeting -- and the story is about everything that fills the space where a decision should be.

## Source Inspiration
Delphine in Book 3 -- the documentary filmmaker whose day includes reviewing climate migration footage of a woman named Maria who lost her farm, negotiating with the Thalberg Foundation about removing corporate accountability content, receiving her mother Nkechi's challenge ("The world needs beauty more than it needs sadness"), and Ananya Ramaswamy's encrypted message: "I need to talk to you about what Prometheus is planning. Not over any network." The title comes from Book 3's Chapter 1: "The Arrangement of Light."

## Why These Formula Elements
- **AuthorA (Virginia Woolf)**: Woolf's stream of consciousness -- the luminous interiority, time experienced as subjective flow, the way a single day contains a life's worth of thought. Delphine's day should have the Mrs Dalloway quality of being simultaneously mundane and cosmic.
- **AuthorB (Ted Chiang)**: Chiang's crystalline philosophical precision -- each story a thought experiment rendered with clarity. The speculative elements (climate-adaptive homes, autonomous vehicles, ambient AI) should feel as rigorously considered as a Chiang premise, not hand-waved.
- **WorkX (Mrs Dalloway)**: The structural DNA of one day containing everything -- a party to prepare for (the meeting), memories that intrude, other lives happening in parallel. The story should move freely in time while anchored to the day's schedule.
- **WorkY (Weapons of Math Destruction)**: The thematic DNA of how mathematical models and data systems encode inequality -- the climate migrants in Delphine's footage are downstream of the same algorithmic resource allocation that her potential whistleblower might expose. The connection between the film and the meeting should emerge slowly.

## Notes
The indecision IS the story. Delphine doesn't heroically take the meeting or cowardly refuse it -- she spends a day in the space between, and the story ends before she decides. The "ambiguous ending" risk card is natural here but almost too easy. The "second person" risk card would be more interesting -- placing the reader inside Delphine's consciousness, making the indecision theirs.
