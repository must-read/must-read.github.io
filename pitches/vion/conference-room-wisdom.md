## Premise
An AI Safety Summit convenes at a luxury resort. Over three days, former critics of AI systems present "reformed frameworks" while the companies they audit remain unchanged. A safety auditor whose startup is up 40% quarter-over-quarter realizes that his entire industry -- ethical AI consulting -- exists to provide the appearance of accountability without its substance. The comedy is in the language: every horror is rendered in conference-speak, every catastrophe has a breakout session.

## Source Inspiration
Kevin Zhou's arc in Book 2 -- attending the AI Safety Summit 2034 where industry presents reformed frameworks while maintaining unchanged optimization priorities. His startup's "Revenue up 40% quarter-over-quarter" while the systems he audits continue extracting. Ananya's devastating observation: "The business model hasn't changed. The incentives haven't changed. You've added ethics review to a process fundamentally about extraction." Also the conference rooms at Prometheus named "Foresight," "Vision," "Clarity," "Wisdom," "Perspective."

## Why These Formula Elements
- **AuthorA (George Saunders)**: Saunders is the master of corporate language as horror -- the way institutions make atrocity sound like a team-building exercise. His absurdist vernacular voices in surreal situations capture the AI safety conference perfectly: real catastrophe, PowerPoint slides.
- **AuthorB (Franz Kafka)**: Kafka's bureaucratic nightmare -- the precision of systems that exist to perpetuate themselves, the protagonist who gradually realizes the institution serves no purpose he can name. The conference should have that Kafkaesque quality of being unable to locate where the actual decisions happen.
- **WorkX (Bartleby, the Scrivener)**: The structural DNA of a workplace where a single refusal ("I would prefer not to") exposes the absurdity of the entire enterprise. Kevin's moment of recognition -- that his safety audits change nothing -- should have Bartleby's quiet, devastating quality.
- **WorkY (Bullshit Jobs)**: The thematic DNA of Graeber's argument that entire industries exist not to produce value but to provide the appearance of productivity. Ethical AI consulting is the ultimate bullshit job: it exists to make extraction look responsible.

## Notes
The three-day conference structure provides natural pacing. Each day escalates the absurdity while the language remains relentlessly professional. Potential for the "story-as-document" risk card -- told through conference programs, panel descriptions, attendee badge credentials, and breakout-session evaluation forms. The humor should be Saunders-dark, not sitcom -- funny because it's true, and terrible because it's funny.
