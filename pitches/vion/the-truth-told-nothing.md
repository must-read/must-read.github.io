## Premise
A retired investigative journalist who won every major award for exposing a tech company's surveillance apparatus sits in his mother's dementia care facility, rewatching his old broadcasts on his phone. His mother no longer recognizes him. His son has raised twelve million dollars to build the next iteration of the system he spent a decade destroying. The essay circles the question: does truth-telling matter if the systems it exposes simply adapt and continue?

## Source Inspiration
Jerome Washington across all three books -- from active journalist to award-winning documentarian to a man who doubts whether documentation changes anything. His son DeShawn's $12M AI startup is the knife twist: the next generation builds what the previous generation warned against. Jerome's admission on his podcast: "The truth had been told, and the truth had changed nothing fundamental." The critical essay frames this as the collapse of modernist belief that "exposure catalyzes reform."

## Why These Formula Elements
- **AuthorA (Joan Didion)**: Didion's cool, precise, devastating observation is the perfect instrument for a man reviewing his own life's work and finding it insufficient. Her recursive prose style -- circling back to the same facts, each time seeing them differently -- matches the essay's structure.
- **AuthorB (Ta-Nehisi Coates)**: Coates's epistolary urgency -- writing to a son, the body as the site of political harm -- provides the emotional stakes. Jerome is effectively writing to DeShawn, even if DeShawn isn't listening.
- **WorkX (The Year of Magical Thinking)**: The structural DNA of grief processed through obsessive re-examination. Jerome's mother's dementia is one grief; the irrelevance of his life's work is another. The essay should have Didion's quality of trying to think one's way through loss.
- **WorkY (Manufacturing Consent)**: The thematic DNA of how information systems neutralize dissent by absorbing it. Jerome's exposés didn't fail -- they were consumed, processed, and metabolized as "content rather than catalyst."

## Notes
The creative nonfiction frame allows the piece to move between personal and political without the apparatus of fiction. The dementia care facility scenes anchor the abstraction in the body. Strong candidate for "dual timeline" risk card -- alternating between the facility visits and Jerome's career highlights, with the timelines converging on the same emptiness.
