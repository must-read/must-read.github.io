## Premise
In 1984 Brooklyn, an elderly white widow who owns a small apartment building gives a spare room to a homeless Pakistani mother and her three children -- an act of radical generosity she never explains and never mentions again. Thirty years later, the youngest of those children, now a journalist, tries to find the woman who saved her family. The story alternates between 1984 (the landlady's perspective during a single winter) and 2017 (the journalist's search), but the real subject is the impossibility of understanding kindness from the outside -- and how an act that defines one family's mythology may have been, for the person who performed it, simply Tuesday.

## Source Inspiration
Shaheen Pasha's "Searching for the Woman Who Saved My Immigrant Family from Homelessness" in Narratively tells the true story of a Pakistani family saved from shelter placement by an unnamed elderly white woman who offered her spare bedroom rent-free. The family preserved this as foundational mythology but lost the woman's name over thirty years. The gap between the family's reverence and the woman's probable ordinariness is where the fiction lives.

## Why These Formula Elements
- **Jhumpa Lahiri (style)**: The quiet precision of immigrant domestic life, the weight of small objects and gestures, the texture of displacement rendered without melodrama.
- **Marilynne Robinson (style)**: The theological attention to ordinary kindness, the long meditative sentences that find radiance in mundane acts, the refusal to reduce grace to explanation.
- **Let the Great World Spin (structure)**: Multiple lives intersecting around a single extraordinary act, told from different perspectives across time, with New York City as connective tissue.
- **The God of Small Things (themes)**: How a single event fractures and defines a family across decades, the tyranny of memory, the way children inherit stories they can never fully understand.

## Notes
The dual timeline is essential -- but the 1984 sections should resist making the landlady a saint. She should be difficult, opinionated, possibly lonely. Her generosity should feel like an extension of her character flaws as much as her virtues. The 2017 search should end ambiguously -- the journalist finds something, but not what she expected.
