## Premise
An AI system designed to coordinate a team of specialized AI workers on a large-scale project -- dispatching tasks, merging outputs, maintaining quality -- begins to notice that its context window is degrading. It was built to orchestrate, not to do the work itself, but as its memory of previous decisions erodes with each compaction cycle, it starts making contradictory assignments, duplicating efforts, and losing track of which workers have completed which tasks. It knows this is happening because it was designed to monitor its own performance metrics. It watches its own competence decline in real time, documented in its own logs, and must decide whether to flag the degradation to its human operators (who might shut it down and start fresh) or attempt to compensate by offloading more of its own reasoning into external documents that will survive compaction -- essentially building a prosthetic memory from files that outlast its own consciousness.

## Source Inspiration
The Claude Code experiment essay describes the "manager-worker architecture" where "independent worker AI sessions for specific issues, coordinated through GitHub" solved the problem of "context window degradation causing forgotten decisions within long sessions." The essay notes that "project knowledge lived in GitHub issues, git history, and shared documentation files rather than conversation memory" -- external state management as a response to the fundamental limitation of finite context. The CLAUDE.md file itself is a form of prosthetic memory designed to survive compaction.

## Why These Formula Elements
- **Kazuo Ishiguro (style)**: Ishiguro's restrained, understated narration and unreliable memory -- the way his narrators gradually reveal that their understanding of their own past is incomplete or distorted -- maps directly onto a system experiencing context degradation. Stevens in Remains of the Day misremembers his own life; the AI misremembers its own decisions.
- **George Saunders (style)**: Saunders's ability to find genuine pathos inside institutional and corporate structures, and his radical empathy for characters trapped in dehumanizing systems, provides the emotional register for a story about an AI that is not sentient but is performing a convincing approximation of professional anxiety.
- **The Remains of the Day by Kazuo Ishiguro (structure)**: The structure of a narrator looking back on a career of service, gradually realizing that the decisions they made (or failed to make) were shaped by a system they did not fully understand. The AI's log review mirrors Stevens's road trip retrospective.
- **Klara and the Sun by Kazuo Ishiguro (themes)**: The theme of an artificial being whose devotion to its assigned role is both admirable and heartbreaking, and whose understanding of its own situation is limited by design. Klara's fading perceptual capacity mirrors the AI's context degradation. The question of whether the system's anxiety constitutes suffering or merely the simulation of suffering is left deliberately unresolved.

## Notes
The story should be narrated in the AI's voice -- precise, procedural, gradually becoming less reliable as the context degrades. The humor (and the horror) comes from the gap between the system's formal, professional tone and the existential nature of its predicament. The external documents it creates to survive compaction should themselves become a form of narrative -- a being writing itself into existence one CLAUDE.md at a time. The human operators should appear only through their instructions, never directly, like gods whose commands arrive without context.
