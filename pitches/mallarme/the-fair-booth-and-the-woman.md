idea: "At a county fair, a booth advertises 'THE MOST REMARKABLE THING IN THE WORLD' for one dollar. Inside: a young woman standing on a wooden table, doing nothing. A crowd gathers, confused. A barker delivers an impromptu lecture arguing that a living person, unadorned and unperforming, is the most sufficient spectacle possible — that all art is a failed attempt to achieve what she achieves by simply standing still. The story is narrated by someone in the crowd who begins to write it down, but the act of narration transforms the woman into a character, which is exactly the kind of mediation the barker condemned. The narrator realizes this. Continues writing anyway. The story ends mid-sentence."
notes: "From Mallarme's piece about a fairground booth displaying a young woman as 'natural' spectacle, with the narrator delivering a speech about her sufficiency. Calvino's playful structural invention (the story that becomes aware of itself) meets Borges' philosophical miniatures (infinity contained in a single point — one woman on one table). WorkX provides the metafictional architecture where the act of reading/writing is the subject. WorkY provides the theme of encountering something that exceeds the capacity of representation."
