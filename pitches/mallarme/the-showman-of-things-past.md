idea: "An aging curator operates a traveling exhibition of 'living tableaux' — real women posed as famous paintings, displayed in department store windows across a declining Midwestern city. The narrator, a poet hired to write catalog copy, becomes obsessed with one exhibit: a woman embodying Manet's Olympia who seems to exist outside time. The story charts the exhibition's slow failure as audiences lose interest, the curator's increasingly desperate salesmanship, and the poet's growing suspicion that the woman in the window is not performing anything — she is genuinely preserved from another era. Never confirmed. The exhibition closes. The windows go dark."
notes: "From Mallarme's piece about a carnival showman displaying a naked woman from a previous era as living artifact. Carter's feminist spectacle and baroque sensuality (the woman as both subject and object of the gaze) meets Ishiguro's quiet devastation and unreliable narration (the poet may be projecting everything). WorkX gives us the theatrical frame and the question of exploitation vs. art. WorkY gives us the theme of a life spent in service to a declining institution, the dignity and tragedy of performing one's role to the end."
