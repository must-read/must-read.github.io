idea: "A linguist becomes haunted by a phrase — five syllables in an unknown language — that surfaces unbidden during a conference presentation. Over three days the phrase reshapes her perception: she begins navigating the city as though guided by its rhythm, arriving at locations that feel predetermined. A luthier's workshop. A taxidermist's back room. A church where the organist plays a melody built on the same five-note pattern. The story never explains the phrase or confirms anything supernatural, but the linguist's rational framework dissolves completely."
notes: "From Mallarme's prose poem about the phrase 'The Penultimate is dead' that possesses the narrator against his will. The horror here is quiet and epistemological — language itself becomes uncanny. Aickman's 'strange story' ambiguity (is this supernatural or psychological?) meets Lispector's philosophical interiority (a consciousness being reorganized from within). WorkX provides the structure of creeping, inexorable change. WorkY provides the theme of a rational person confronting something that dismantles their categories of understanding."
