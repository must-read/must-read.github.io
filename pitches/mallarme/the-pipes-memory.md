idea: "A middle-aged translator, packing to leave her London flat after twenty years, finds an old briar pipe belonging to her former lover in a drawer. She hasn't smoked in decades, but she fills and lights it. The tobacco smell reconstructs an entire winter: the year she arrived in London, the coal-smoke fogs, the ferry crossings to Calais, the cramped bedsit where she first attempted to translate Nerval. The story spirals through layered memory — each sensory detail opening another chamber — until the translator realizes she is reconstructing not the past but a version of herself that no longer exists. She finishes the pipe. She continues packing."
notes: "From Mallarme's piece about rediscovering an abandoned pipe that triggers vivid memories of a year in London. Sebald's digressive, walking-and-remembering prose (photographs of things that no longer exist, places layered with loss) meets Woolf's stream of consciousness (time as subjective flow, a single day containing an entire life). WorkX provides the structure of wandering association, where one memory leads to another across geography and time. WorkY provides the architecture of a single present-tense action (packing/walking through London) that contains the past in compressed form."
